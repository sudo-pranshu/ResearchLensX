# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard
Be respectful and constructive. Criticise code, methods and results, not people. Welcome newcomers, assume good faith, and credit others' work. Scientific disagreement is encouraged; it must be backed by evidence and stated civilly.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, sexualised language or imagery, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope
This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public representation of the project.

## Enforcement
The maintainer, Pranshu Kumar, is responsible for enforcement and may edit or remove contributions, and temporarily or permanently block contributors who violate this code. Report concerns privately through GitHub: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/sudo-pranshu/ResearchLensX/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
